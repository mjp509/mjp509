Privacy policy

itsmotch VODs is a personal site that shows past itsmotch Twitch streams with chat replay.

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, sign-ups, forms or analytics.

Stored in your browser

Your place in each VOD is saved in your own browser (local storage) for "Continue watching". It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Third-party content

Videos play through YouTube, and the live player and clips through Twitch. Those services may set their own cookies under their own policies: Google/YouTube and Twitch. Chat replay shows public Twitch chat messages from past streams.

YouTube API

The site's automation uses read-only access to the itsmotch YouTube channel to find uploaded VODs. It does not access any visitor's Google account.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: message itsmotch on Twitch.
